About this website

Clear information. Realistic plans.

Canada Pathway NG helps Nigerians research Canadian work, study, and permanent-residence options using practical explanations and official sources.

Our purpose

Relocation research can be confusing. People may encounter outdated advice, expensive promises, and fake job offers. Our goal is to make the first steps easier to understand so readers can ask better questions and verify information before spending money.

What we publish

  • Plain-language explainers about Canadian immigration pathways.
  • Job-search guidance for candidates applying from outside Canada.
  • Budget and document-planning checklists.
  • Scam-awareness guidance and links to official resources.

How we handle accuracy

Immigration rules, programme eligibility, fees, processing times, and financial requirements can change. We link to official Canadian government sources where possible and encourage readers to check the current instructions before making decisions. If a page becomes outdated, it should be reviewed and corrected.

Independence notice: Canada Pathway NG is not affiliated with the Government of Canada, Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC), an employer, a school, or a recruitment agency.

What we do not do

We do not sell job offers, issue visas, recruit workers, guarantee approvals, or provide legal representation. Information on this website is general educational material, not individual immigration advice.
