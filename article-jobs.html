How to search for Canadian jobs from Nigeria

Focus on vacancies that consider candidates outside Canada, prepare evidence of your skills, and understand the work-permit process before paying anyone.

Updated: 13 October 2026

Step 1: Understand the permit before applying

Most people applying from outside Canada cannot simply get an open work permit. An employer-specific permit usually requires a job offer, and the employer may need an LMIA unless an exemption applies. A job advertisement is not the same as permission to work in Canada.

Check which work permit you may need

Step 2: Use official job-search resources

Canada Job Bank has a page for foreign candidates and explains how to identify employers recruiting internationally. Read the posting carefully: some jobs are open only to people already authorised to work in Canada, while others may consider applicants from outside the country.

Search Job Bank as a foreign candidate

Step 3: Prepare a truthful, targeted CV

  • Use clear job titles and dates.
  • Describe responsibilities and measurable achievements relevant to the vacancy.
  • Include certificates, licences, and training only when you actually have them.
  • Do not claim you already have Canadian work authorisation if you do not.
  • Check whether the occupation requires a licence or credential recognition.

Step 4: Verify the employer and offer

Look up the company independently using its official website and contact details. Confirm the role, location, pay, duties, and application process. Be suspicious if someone uses only a personal messaging account, refuses a video or formal interview, sends a contract with inconsistent details, or demands money urgently.

Step 5: Understand who pays which fees

Employers have their own legal obligations when hiring foreign workers. Be cautious if a recruiter tells you to pay for a guaranteed job offer or to buy an LMIA. Do not send money based only on a document or screenshot. Verify the rules on official government pages and report suspected fraud through appropriate official channels.

Before you submit an application

  1. Save a copy of the original job advertisement.
  2. Check the employer's identity and business details independently.
  3. Keep copies of your CV, application, and correspondence.
  4. Confirm the current work-permit document list and whether an LMIA or exemption applies.
  5. Never submit fake experience letters or altered documents.

International hiring is real, but not every employer is recruiting abroad and no job offer guarantees a work permit.
