Budget planning

Planning Canada relocation costs on a limited budget

Before paying application fees, school deposits, or an agent, estimate the full cost of your intended pathway and verify the current rules.

Updated: 13 October 2026
Important: There is no universal “cheap Canada visa.” Required funds vary by programme, family size, location, and current rules. Do not rely on old social-media figures.

For a study route

Study-permit applicants must show they can pay tuition, living expenses, and transportation for themselves and accompanying family members, where applicable. A scholarship that covers tuition may not cover living costs or travel. You should also plan for application fees, tests, credential documents, deposits, and emergency funds where relevant.

The Government of Canada updates proof-of-funds requirements. Check the official page for the amount and rules that apply on the date you apply.

For a work route

Costs may include document preparation, language or credential assessments if required, medical examinations or biometrics where applicable, travel, and money to support yourself before your first paycheque. The exact list depends on the permit and your circumstances. Confirm official fees and instructions before budgeting.

Official guide: applying for a work permit from outside Canada

For permanent residence

Economic immigration routes can involve language tests, educational credential assessments, application fees, medical examinations, biometrics, translations, and proof of settlement funds where required. Some expenses occur before you know whether you will receive an invitation or approval, so compare costs and eligibility before beginning.

A simple budget worksheet

  1. List each required application and assessment fee from its official page.
  2. Add document translation, testing, medical, and credential costs that apply to you.
  3. Estimate airfare and the first weeks or months of living costs.
  4. For a study plan, separate tuition from proof-of-funds requirements and travel.
  5. Keep an emergency buffer; do not spend all available cash on an agent or deposit.
  6. Convert Canadian-dollar estimates to naira using a current rate, then allow for exchange-rate changes.

Do not fake proof of funds

Do not use a temporary borrowed balance, fabricated bank statement, or misleading sponsorship letter to appear eligible. The evidence must be truthful and meet the current instructions. False documents can lead to refusal and other serious consequences.
